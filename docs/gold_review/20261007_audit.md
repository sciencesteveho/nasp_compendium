and evaluation tiers; it does not mean all 159 relationships are biologically
false. Unresolved claims remain outside the candidate denominators.

Different contexts can support different signs; the Gulen IFNAR co-cultures illustrate why evidence must stay attached to each claim.

- **Martinez:** the supplied main PDF is the October 2024 preprint. Its supplementary figures were retrieved through the matching Europe PMC record; no later article version was silently substituted. Male survival and STING-loss specificity qualifications are in the notes.
- **Vocabulary:** candidate proposals remain unapproved. Review the list below, reuse an existing canonical term when biologically identical, and update candidates/references together before freezing evaluation.

These are **external information gaps** or **decisions**, not software failures. Fresh source-isolated extraction quality remains an **unresolved core**: ratify references and run the paired holdout protocol before claiming the refactor reliably extracts biology.
