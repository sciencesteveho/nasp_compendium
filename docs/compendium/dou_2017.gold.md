    summary: "Lamin B1 loss during senescence allows cytoplasmic chromatin fragments (CCF) to form and recruit CGAS, which produces cGAMP (LC-MS-confirmed) and activates STING1, driving RELA/NF-kB-mediated SASP. The same axis runs chronically in OIS-evaded and cancer cells and correlates with SASP gene expression across CCLE and TCGA; the cytosolic DNA sensor CGAS, not IFI16, drives the response."

      1. Tissue/organ: primary human fibroblasts (IMR90, BJ) in vitro; mouse
      liver in ionizing-radiation and NRasV12 hepatocyte models; pan-cancer
      correlations in CCLE (n=1036) and TCGA (pancreatic, melanoma, prostate,
      breast).
      2. Species: human and mouse.
      3. Age range or comparison: cellular senescence models rather than
      organismal aging cohorts; mouse experiments used 8-12 week-old adults,
      WT vs Sting1-null.
      4. Sex-specific effects: both sexes included in mouse studies; not
      addressed.
      5. Disease context: senescence-associated inflammation, immunosurveillance
      of pre-malignant hepatocytes, and pan-cancer inflammatory gene expression.
      Mechanistic spine (all core): LMNB1 loss releases cytoplasmic chromatin
      fragments; CCF recruit CGAS; CGAS makes cGAMP (LC-MS); cGAMP activates
      STING1; STING1 activates RELA (S536 phosphorylation, nuclear
      translocation, promoter occupancy); RELA drives the SASP; and STING1 is
      required in vivo for immunosurveillance. The DNA-branch specificity claim
      (IFI16 does not drive the SASP; MAVS does not track it) is part of the
      core attribution.
      6. Why NF-kB and not IFN: p38_MAPK and IL1A both suppress IFNB1, biasing
      the output to NF-kB/SASP (grouped as ifnb1_suppression). Entry into
      senescence via oncogenic RAS, DNA damage, or replicative exhaustion are
      interchangeable triggers (grouped as senescence_trigger). Pan-cancer
      STING1/CGAS correlation with SASP is one cohort claim (grouped as
      pancancer_pathway_corr). Forbidden shortcuts: naming CGAS or CCF as the
      direct driver of the SASP while dropping the cGAMP -> STING1 -> RELA spine.
    relevance_to_project: >
      Anchors the DNA-sensing (CCF/CGAS-STING) arm of the NASP spine and its
      NF-kB (not IFN) output, shared with De Cecco (L1 cDNA) and complementary
      to López-Polo (mt-dsRNA/MAVS). In atlas or perturbation data, treat CCF
      burden and CGAS-STING-RELA activity as the DNA-branch readout of NASP,
      use the IFI16/MAVS negatives to attribute the signal to the cGAS arm, and
      treat p38/IL1A as brakes that explain a SASP-high but IFN-low state.
