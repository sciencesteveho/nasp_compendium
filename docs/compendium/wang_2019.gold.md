    cite: "Wang L, Wen M, Cao X. Nuclear hnRNPA2B1 initiates and amplifies the innate immune response to DNA viruses. Science. 2019;365(6454):eaav0758."
    url: "https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aav0758"
    summary: "The heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein hnRNPA2B1 is a nuclear DNA sensor. On DNA-virus infection it binds viral genomic DNA in the nucleus, homodimerizes, and is demethylated at Arg226 by the arginine demethylase JMJD6; it then translocates to the cytoplasm where it activates the TBK1-IRF3 pathway through the tyrosine kinase Src to initiate STING-dependent IFN-alpha/beta. hnRNPA2B1 additionally amplifies the response by binding CGAS, IFI16 and STING mRNAs and promoting their m6A modification and nucleocytoplasmic trafficking/translation. The effect is specific to DNA viruses and to type-I IFN, not to RNA viruses or to IL-6/TNF-alpha."

    notes: >
      1. Tissue/organ: mouse and human myeloid/fibroblast lines (RAW264.7, PMs,
      BMDMs, THP1, L929, MEFs, HEK293) in vitro; myeloid-conditional
      Hnrnpa2b1-KO mice (brain viral titer, serum IFN-beta, survival) in vivo.
      2. Species: human and mouse; the hnRNPA2B1-TBK1 interaction is conserved.
      3. Age range or comparison: not an aging study; comparisons are
      Hnrnpa2b1 wild-type vs knockdown/knockout under DNA-virus (HSV-1, AdV)
      versus RNA-virus (VSV, SeV) challenge.
      4. Sex-specific effects: not addressed.
      5. Disease context: innate antiviral IFN-I defense against nuclear DNA
      viruses; relevant by extension to cGAS-STING-linked type-I IFN output.
      Spine: nuclear hnRNPA2B1 binds viral DNA (via its N-terminal RRMs);
      infection induces hnRNPA2B1 homodimerization; JMJD6 demethylates
      hnRNPA2B1 at Arg226; demethylated hnRNPA2B1 translocates to the cytoplasm
      and, through Src, activates TBK1; TBK1 activates IRF3; IRF3 drives
      type-I IFN (IFN-alpha/beta); the IFN induction is STING-dependent.
      Amplification branch: hnRNPA2B1 binds CGAS, IFI16 (p204) and STING mRNAs
      and promotes their m6A modification and nucleocytoplasmic trafficking and
      translation, amplifying cytoplasmic sensor signaling; FTO (an m6A
      demethylase constitutively bound to hnRNPA2B1) restrains this and its
      knockdown raises IFN. Key negatives: hnRNPA2B1 loss does NOT reduce IL-6
      or TNF-alpha, and does NOT affect RNA-virus-induced IFN; hnRNPA2B1 can
      induce IFN partly cGAS-independently (works in Cgas-/- cells).
      A2B1 = hnRNPA2B1; p204 = mouse ortholog of IFI16; Arg226 lies in the RGG
      domain; dimer-interface residues Pro81/Lys82/Arg83/Val172/Arg173/Lys174.
    relevance_to_project: >
      Supplies a second nuclear sensor on the type-I IFN spine, distinct from
      the RPSA/NF-kB proinflammatory route in the same corpus, and a two-layer
      mechanism (direct TBK1-IRF3 initiation plus post-transcriptional
      amplification of the canonical cGAS/IFI16/STING sensors). It is a
      calibration case for (a) keeping a long PTM-gated activation spine intact
      rather than shortcutting to "sensor drives IFN", (b) distinguishing
      initiation from amplification of the same output, and (c) representing an
      induced negative regulator (FTO). In atlas data, treat HNRNPA2B1 as a
      candidate amplifier co-expressed with CGAS/IFI16/STING1 whose level may
      set the gain of the IFN-I response.
