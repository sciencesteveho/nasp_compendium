    cite: "Ma D, Yang M, Wang Q, et al. Arginine methyltransferase PRMT5 negatively regulates cGAS-mediated antiviral immune response. Sci Adv. 2021;7(13):eabc1834."
    url: "https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abc1834"
    summary: "The arginine methyltransferase PRMT5 directly binds cGAS (PRMT5 MTase domain to cGAS N-terminal domain) and catalyzes symmetric dimethylation of cGAS at the Arg124 residue in an RGG/RG motif. This methylation reduces the dsDNA-binding ability of cGAS and thereby suppresses cGAS-cGAMP-STING-TBK1-IRF3 signaling and type-I IFN production upon DNA-virus (HSV-1) infection. PRMT5 acts specifically at cGAS upstream of cGAMP: it does not interact with STING/TBK1/IRF3 and cGAMP stimulation bypasses the suppression. The effect requires PRMT5 catalytic activity (abolished by the E444Q dead mutant), the Arg124 site (R124K mutant resists methylation and restores signaling), and cGAS itself (lost in Cgas-/- cells); PRMT5 inhibitors (EPZ015666, GSK3326595) enhance IFN-beta and protect mice against HSV-1 in a cGAS-dependent manner."

    relevance_to_project: >
      A methylation-suppression / restriction-style case on the canonical
      cGAS-STING spine: a post-translational modification (symmetric arginine
      dimethylation at a defined residue) gates sensor competence by blocking
      dsDNA binding. It is a calibration case for (a) representing a negative
      regulator that acts by a specific PTM at a specific residue without
      shortcutting "PRMT5 suppresses IFN", (b) encoding upstream-of-cGAMP
      specificity via the cGAMP-bypass negative and the lack of downstream
      interaction, and (c) recording the catalytic (E444Q), site (R124K), and
      sensor (Cgas-/-) dependencies that pin the mechanism. High PRMT5 activity
      co-occurring with low cGAS output nominates a methylation-restricted
      sensing state; PRMT5 is a candidate perturbation target for de-repressing
      nucleic-acid sensing.

    source: PRMT5
    target: CGAS
    rel: inhibits
    evidence_strength: direct_measured
    context: "PRMT5 catalyzes symmetric dimethylation (sDMA) of cGAS at Arg124
